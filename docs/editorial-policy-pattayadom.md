# Editorial Policy — PattayaDom (stub)

> Fill during Этап 0. This is the source of truth for what Content Loop may generate for PattayaDom.

## Voice & audience

- Audience: expats / travelers / investors in Pattaya, RU primary.
- Tone: direct, practical, no fluff. Checklist > narrative for how-tos.

## Content types

- Visa / legal how-tos (high-risk)
- Real estate explainers (high-risk)
- Area / lifestyle guides (low-risk)
- News digests (medium-risk)

## Required sources

- Visa/immigration: Thai Immigration Bureau, MFA Thailand, or embassy page. No Telegram rumors.
- Real estate prices/fees: listing or developer source + disclaimer.
- Every high-risk claim must have ≥1 verifiable source URL stored in `source` table.

## Forbidden claims (examples)

- Guaranteed visa approval / guaranteed rental yield / "no risk".
- Legal advice phrasing ("you must" without source); use "generally / typically" + source.
- Unverified price or tax numbers.

## SEO / structure

- Title 45–65 chars, excerpt 120–160 chars.
- Canonical Markdown; adapter renders to FamilyOS HTML.

## Approval

- High-risk: per-material human approval required. No auto-publish.
- Low-risk: batch approval allowed post-pilot only.
